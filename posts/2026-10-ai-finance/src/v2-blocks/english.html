A paragraph to loop

Software already runs much of the market, but running is by no means the same as answering for it. Provided that many firms rely on one model, it can become a single point of failure, and their errors may move in lockstep. Most firms still want a human in the loop, partly because someone has to be held accountable. A token counts as money only as long as a community accepts it. What remains to be seen is whether machines could ever be that community. Standard contracts cut both ways. Don’t read too much into a toy, with the caveat that it shows what could happen, not what will.

Four sentence patterns that sound C1
Reporting verbs
“The IMF judges the risks currently contained; the FSB warns that common models could correlate trading; Gensler and Bailey hypothesise a monoculture; Searle argues that status needs acceptance.” Match the verb to how firm the claim is.
Three layers
“The source says X. The evidence shows Y. Our reading is Z.”
Hedging ladder
shows → suggests → is consistent with → hints at.
Concession, then your point
“Granted, the toy is simple. But the threshold is real.”
Small fixes that make a big difference
if-clause without will
“If the models fail, …”, not “will fail”.
evidence (uncountable)
“some evidence”, not “evidences”.
general nouns, no article
“society”, “finance”, but “the financial system”.
