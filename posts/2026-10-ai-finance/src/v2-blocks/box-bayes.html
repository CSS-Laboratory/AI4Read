Look inside: counting one voice ten times

Instance. Prior: 1 in 2 that a bank is safe. A safe bank is 4 times as likely to get a “safe” verdict as a risky one (the likelihood ratio; invented numbers). Mechanism. Each independent verdict multiplies the odds by 4 (the odds form of Bayes’ rule).

\[\begin{aligned}\text{posterior odds}&=\text{prior odds}\times 4^{\,n}\\P&=\frac{\text{odds}}{1+\text{odds}}\end{aligned}\]

Worked numbers. Counted \(n=0,\dots,4\) times: \(P(\text{safe})=0.5,\,0.8,\,0.941,\,0.985,\,0.996\). Ten times: odds 1,048,576, \(P=0.999999\). Check. \(n=1\): \(4/5=0.8\).

If ten banks run copies of one model, their ten verdicts are one verdict. Counting it ten times turns 0.8 into 0.999999: ten copies of one voice feel like certainty.
