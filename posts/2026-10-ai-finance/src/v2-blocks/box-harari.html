All of Harari’s lines we use, by group, with timestamps

1. Asserts: claims with no hedge

  • “It is not just another tool. It is an agent” WEF 2026 [2:00]; “AI is a knife that can invent new kinds of knives as well as new kinds of music, medicine and money” [2:47]
  • “As far as putting words in order is concerned, AI already thinks better than many of us” WEF 2026 [5:48]; “Therefore, anything made of words will be taken over by AI” [5:57]; “If laws are made of words, then AI will take over the legal system” [6:06]; “Everything made of words will be taken over by AI” [10:36]
  • “the first tool that we created that can actually understand finance better than us”; “It’s only data, it’s only information” BIS 2024 [5:14], [5:27]
  • “the first tool in history that can make decisions by itself and can create new ideas by itself” [16:21]
  • “it is already very creative”: his example is AlphaGo, a Go program, not an LLM and not finance [25:14]–[25:57]; he extends it to science and art [27:04] and to finance as “a sort of art” [30:07]
  • Finance is “the ideal field for AI because it’s only data” Guardian 2023
  • “The third thing to know about AI is that it can lie and manipulate”; “AIs have already learned how to lie” WEF 2026 [2:58], [3:26]
  • AI brings “a very large number of dangerous scenarios, each of them having a relatively small probability that taken together … constitutes an existential threat to the survival of human civilisation” Guardian 2023
  • “What gives money value is the stories that bankers, finance ministers and cryptocurrency gurus tell us about it” Ynet 2023; “money is really trust” BIS 2024 [23:15]

2. Predicts: forecasts without a hedge

  • “I think we’ll see AI creativity also in this field coming up with new types of financial devices” BIS 2024 [30:24]
  • “We need to prepare for the moment … when AIs create a new financial system that they understand and we don’t understand” WEF 2026 [31:11]
  • AIs “will soon be able to make the decisions necessary to manage a bank account” and “can therefore function as persons” WEF 2026 [16:28], [16:36]. The forecast is group 2; his next sentence, “Do we want to allow that?” [16:47], is a policy question.
  • “Ten years from now, it will be too late for you to decide whether AIs should function as persons in the financial markets, in the courts, in the churches” [19:11]; “Somebody else will already have decided it for you” [19:23]
  • On himself as a writer: “AI will beat me. I don’t know how long it will take. Two years, five years, ten years, it will beat me” [26:29]–[26:35] (the outcome is asserted; the timing is explicitly unknown)

3. Imagines, supposes, asks: hedged

  • “Now imagine a situation maybe in 10 years when AIs create a new financial device which is orders of magnitude more complicated than CDOs” BIS 2024 [9:54]; it works for “a couple of years” [10:19], then “something goes wrong and nobody knows why” [10:27]
  • “Suppose some … AI persons invent super efficient and super complex financial devices that humans cannot fully understand” WEF 2026 [17:29]
  • “Maybe nobody in the room, no human in the room, understands the financial system anymore” [31:59] (the Q&A scenario, “ten years from now, Davos” [31:54])
  • A hedged estimate about today: “maybe 1% of the population really understands how the financial system works” BIS 2024 [3:00]. Then the what-if: “What happens if this number goes down to zero?” [3:09]; “It could lead to power shifting from humans to algorithms” [3:30]
  • 2023: “What happens if … it starts creating new financial devices that only AI can understand, that no human being can understand?” Guardian 2023
  • 2024: “imagine the following scenario”, an AI called Broomstick and a crash “bigger even than 1929 or 2008” that no human can explain Guardian 2024

4. Concedes, proposes: his limits and remedies

  • On thinking: “At the present moment, we still think better” WEF 2026 [30:55], just before his forecast about a new financial system [31:11]
  • “at least for now we have zero evidence that AIs can feel anything” WEF 2026 [8:07]; humans’ place “depends on the place we assign our non-verbal feelings” [11:39]
  • “we don’t need everybody to understand everything, this is really impossible” BIS 2024 [9:21], before he turns to 2008
  • “history is never deterministic” BIS 2024 [20:46]; “humans are extremely adaptable” [12:42]; institutions [21:37]; “we’ll need to keep humans in the loop” [22:56]
  • An AI-made financial crisis is “a catastrophic risk”, but “by itself I wouldn’t describe it as existential”; “It could, indirectly, if it triggers certain kinds of wars or conflicts” Guardian 2023
  • “Computers are not yet powerful enough to completely escape our control” and “As long as humanity stands united, we can build institutions that will regulate AI”, followed at once by “Unfortunately, humanity has never been united” and “The rise of AI poses an existential danger to humankind, not because of the malevolence of computers, but because of our own shortcomings” Guardian 2024. The remedy comes with his doubt that we will use it.

Trap · at least four different “ten years”

(1) BIS: “maybe in 10 years”, an AI-made device (group 3). (2) Davos Q&A: “ten years from now, Davos”, whether anyone in the room understands finance (group 3). (3) Davos talk: “Ten years from now, it will be too late” to decide on AI persons, a deadline for a decision (group 2). (4) Davos Q&A on himself: “two years, five years, ten years”, with “I don’t know how long it will take”. Never merge them.
