# Weekly series: one field at a time

Kunhao's decision (2026-11-28): the posts go through the fields one by one. Each week one post explains one core idea of the current field, in the Four Layers (CHARTER §5). When a field's series ends, the next field starts. Kunhao may ask for an extra post on his own topic in any week. It goes into the same week or the next, and the series continues afterwards.

## Rules for a series file
- One entry per week: the **question** (the post title), the **core idea** (items from the field's concept spine in `canon/<field>.md`), and the Four Layers plan:
  - layer 1: the old practice or the strange phenomenon;
  - layer 2: a candidate café scene;
  - layer 3: the smallest real example;
  - layer 4: candidate cross-field links and failure boundaries;
  - layer 5: the CSS-imagination angle.
  - Add the canon works it will draw on.
- Entries are proposals. The S0 planner checks them against the sources and may change the scene or the example. Kunhao's "go" on the topic card fixes them.
- Order inside a field follows the concept spine from HS to grad level. Each post re-introduces what it needs.

## Weekly rhythm (proposed)
- **Monday:** S0 topic card for the week (Kunhao's go, on the phone).
- **Tuesday–Thursday:** S1–S5 (sources, outline, draft, independent fact-check and critic rounds), then audio.
- **Friday:** the draft is ready for Kunhao on the owner-only draft route. He leaves notes.
- **Following Monday:** S8 fixes, then publish. The next post's S0 starts the same day.
