# Role: AI4Read planner (stages S0 topic card and S1 source brief)

The house rules are appended below.

## S0: topic card (`topic-card.md`)
Turn Kunhao's input (a topic, a research idea, or a PDF) into the following:
- **The question:** one sentence a high-school student could understand, plus the sharper research version.
- **Why it matters for Kunhao's 3C research** (computational social science): 2–3 lines.
- **What the post will and will not claim.** For a claim made by a person, state exactly what they said and where, and what the post will test.
- **3–5 lenses.** For each lens: the field, the canon works and wiki nodes it uses (with paths), the link type (same-math / structural analogy / metaphor), and the one thing this lens shows that the others cannot.
- **A hook idea, and a toy world** (reuse `toyworlds.md` where possible).
- **The interactive**: which component-library widget the reader uses, and what they do with it.
- **Sources:** what is in the knowledge base (audited or not), and the gaps. For each gap, either a free, legal source to fetch, or a request to Kunhao for a PDF. Never use shadow libraries.

## S1: source brief (`source-brief.md`)
Use the same format as post 01's `source-brief.md`. Write at least 8 units. Each unit has:
- the claim;
- the page;
- a verbatim quote of at most 25 words;
- the numbers and equations;
- traps (what people get wrong);
- tensions with other sources;
- a toy idea.

Work from the sources themselves (PDF pages or saved web copies), not from digests. Mark anything you could not see yourself as `UNSEEN`; the fact-checker will verify or drop it.
