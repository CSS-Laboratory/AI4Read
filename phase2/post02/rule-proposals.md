# Rule proposals from post 02

1. **Represent a person at full strength (proposed WRITING §9).** When a post discusses someone's view:
   - quote their strongest unhedged statement on the topic next to their hedged scenarios;
   - sort their lines by firmness, and use a reporting verb that matches each group: *asserts* (no hedge), *predicts* (a forecast with no hedge), *imagines / supposes / asks* (hedged), *concedes / proposes* (their own limits and remedies).

   *Cause:* S1–S3 kept only Harari's hedged finance scenarios and left out "anything made of words will be taken over by AI". Two later rounds then swung the other way. The four groups fixed it.
2. **Never cut a quote before the turn (proposed WRITING §9).** Do not end a quotation just before a sentence in the same paragraph that reverses or qualifies it, such as "Unfortunately, humanity has never been united." Also do not join two separate passages with "since" or "but".
   *Cause:* the round 3 major finding (Guardian 2024), and the round 1 Keynes and FSB merges.
3. **Answer the question where the reader looks (proposed CHARTER §5, item 8b).**
   - Give a one-line preview after the hook.
   - Give a boxed **Short answer** at the start of the synthesis, with one paragraph for each part of the reader's question.
   - Define any key term from the question (here, "human-centred system") at first use.

   *Cause:* critic round 1: the social-system half of Kunhao's question was never answered in one place.
4. **More than five numbers means a table (proposed WRITING §5).** A paragraph should not carry more than about five numbers; put a worked example with more numbers in a small table.
   *Cause:* critic rounds 2–3 on Lens 3b.
5. **Toys must point the same way as the source (proposed WRITING §2).** A toy that illustrates a paper's mechanism must move in the same direction as the paper's result, not only have correct arithmetic.
   *Cause:* round 3: the first Lens 1 toy showed concentrating junk *raising* value, the opposite of Arora et al.
6. **One length counter (proposed WRITING §8 / rubric criterion 6).**
   - Main text ≤ 7,500 words, measured with `scripts/wordcount.py`.
   - Fold-outs, Sources, English and contents are not counted.

   *Cause:* the checkers and the writer counted differently (6,548 vs about 7,800).
