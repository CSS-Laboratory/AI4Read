# Harari on AI and finance — verified claims

## Bottom line on Kunhao's paraphrase
"Within ~10 years LLMs may take over the finance system and create new types of finance" is **traceable to Harari, but it merges two talks and makes his wording stronger**:
- **BIS Innovation Summit, 7 May 2024** [9:54]: "Now imagine a situation maybe in 10 years when AIs create a new financial device, which is orders of magnitude more complicated than CDOs". At [30:17]: "I think we'll see AI creativity also in this field coming up with new types of financial security[ies]."
- **WEF Davos, Jan 2026** [31:54–31:59]: "We can be in the same situation, ten years from now … Maybe nobody in the room, no human in the room, understands the financial system anymore, because it's dominated by AIs".
- He says **"AI"/"AIs", never "LLMs"**.
- He frames these as **thought experiments** ("imagine", "maybe", "suppose").
- His worry is **human incomprehension**, which leads to a crisis no one can regulate and to a loss of democratic accountability. It is not simply "AI takes over".
- He calls an AI financial crisis "catastrophic" but "by itself I wouldn't describe it as existential" (Guardian, 9 Nov 2023).
- Meta-manager check (2026-10-17): the BIS [9:54], [30:17] and Davos [31:11–32:12] lines were confirmed in the stored transcripts.

## Critiques
1. **Steven Poole, *Guardian* review of *Nexus*, 11 Sep 2024.** "peculiarly credulous about the capabilities of what is now marketed as 'AI'"; "No one has yet seen a chatbot create new ideas". https://www.theguardian.com/books/article/2024/sep/11/nexus-by-yuval-noah-harari-review-the-ai-apocalypse
2. **Killian Fox, *Observer* review, 10 Sep 2024.** "capitalism is hardly mentioned at all". The actors are profit-seeking firms. https://www.theguardian.com/books/article/2024/sep/10/nexus-a-brief-history-of-information-networks-from-the-stone-age-to-ai-by-yuval-noah-harari-review-rage-against-the-machine
3. **Narayanan & Kapoor, "AI as Normal Technology", Knight Institute, Apr 2025.** Impacts unfold over decades, and humans and organisations stay in control. https://knightcolumbia.org/content/ai-as-normal-technology
4. **FSB, "The Financial Stability Implications of AI", 14 Nov 2024.** The risks are concentration, correlation and herding, cyber, and model risk. "existing financial policy frameworks address many of the vulnerabilities". https://www.fsb.org/uploads/R14112024.pdf
5. **Cecilia Skingsley, in the BIS conversation itself.** People trust aeroplanes they don't understand; products arise to satisfy needs; "I'm more optimistic than you."
6. **Darshana Narayanan, *Current Affairs*, Jul 2022.** A neuroscientist's critique of his scientific reliability (predates *Nexus*). https://currentaffairs.org/2022/07/the-dangerous-populist-science-of-yuval-noah-harari

**Unread leads:** the *Washington Post* review by Justin Smith-Ruiu (Sep 2024); Konch, *AI & Society* 41(2) 2026; the FT op-ed itself.
