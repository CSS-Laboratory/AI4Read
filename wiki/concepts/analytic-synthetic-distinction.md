# analytic-synthetic-distinction

## Canon spine entry

- **analytic / synthetic distinction** (analytic-philosophy): true by meaning alone vs. true because of how the world is; Quine denied there is a principled line (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch02-logic]] (pp. 25–26): true by meaning alone vs. true by meaning plus the world; the positivists held logic and mathematics analytic [as `analytic / synthetic distinction`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch07-lakatos]] (p. 118): Carnap: framework principles appear as analytic sentences; moves inside test synthetic claims [as `analytic / synthetic distinction`]
