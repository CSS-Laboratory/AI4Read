# two-step-flow

## Canon spine entry

- **Two-step flow** (communication-theory): media influence passes through opinion leaders, who relay it to less attentive people (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[habermas-1989-publicsphere/ch06-transformation-p2]] (p. 213): Habermas speaks of a two-tiered communication process mediated by opinion leaders; he does not use the term itself
- [[habermas-1989-publicsphere/ch07-concept]] (p. 242): mentioned as example of psychology-biased communication research
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch00-introduction]] (pp. 3–4): media influence reaches opinion leaders, who pass it to others; reported as something the 1940 study "seemed" to show
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch02-played]] (p. 32): ideas often seem to flow from media to opinion leaders, then to less active people
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch03-essay]] (pp. 44–45): more exposed people pass media content to less exposed contacts; here the basis of the relay function
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch07-group]] (pp. 116–117): relay of media content via interpersonal channels; evidence called modest in quantity
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch14-two]] (p. 309): ideas go from media to opinion leaders and on to the less active; here tested beyond elections
- [[rogers-2003-diffusion/ch08-diffusion-p1]] (pdf 381–382): media → leaders → followers; Rogers calls it oversimplified but useful
