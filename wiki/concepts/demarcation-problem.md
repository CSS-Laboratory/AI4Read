# demarcation-problem

## Canon spine entry

- **demarcation problem** (analytic-philosophy): what separates science from non-science or pseudoscience (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch01-introduction]] (pp. 2–5): here only informally: what separates science (gray areas included) from other inquiry [as `demarcation problem`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch04-popper]] (p. 58): Popper's name for the problem of separating science from non-science, incl. pseudo-science [as `demarcation problem`]
- [[popper-1959-logic/appi-untitled-2]] (p. 314): finding a criterion to distinguish empirical-science statements from metaphysical ones; Popper calls it Kant's problem [as `demarcation problem`]

- [[popper-1959-logic/ch04-falsifiability]] (p. 61): here: separating empirical systems from conventionalist or metaphysical ones, by a logical criterion plus a methodological decision [as `demarcation problem`]
