# priming

## Canon spine entry

- **Priming** (communication-theory): media emphasis changes the criteria people use to evaluate leaders or issues (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[entman-1993-framing/main]] (p. 54): as in Sniderman et al. (quoted): framing primes values differentially, establishing salience of one or another
- [[lazer-2018-fakenews/main]] (p. 8): media typically cannot change attitudes but are far more successful at changing what we think about

### decision-game-theory

- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch11-anchors]] (pdf 121): an idea or number automatically activates compatible thoughts [new]
