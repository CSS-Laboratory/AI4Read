# linear-stability-analysis

## Canon spine entry

- **linear stability analysis** (complex-systems): judging a fixed point's stability from the eigenvalues of the Jacobian (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p3]] (p. 41): Jacobian eigenvalues of the egalitarian state decide hierarchy onset in the Bonabeau model
- [[lorenz-1963-nonperiodic/main]] (pp. 135–136): eigenvalues of (29) at steady states give $r=1$ and (34) [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 55): Box 2 linearizes around an equilibrium; $\lambda$ gives the recovery rate [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch02-flows]] (pp. 24–25): here in 1D: the sign of $f'(x^*)$ decides stability; fails if $f'(x^*)=0$ [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p1]] (p. 48; also p. 57 for the pitchfork at $r=0$): stability from the sign of $f'(x^*)$. At the saddle-node bifurcation point $f'(x^*)=0$: the linearisation vanishes
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch05-linear]] (pp. 132, 138): here only the linear case: type read from eigenvalues, equivalently from $\tau$ and $\Delta$
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch06-phase-p1]] (p. 152): classify a fixed point from the Jacobian eigenvalues; valid if not marginal
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch06-phase-p2]] (p. 174): described as a local method, with its limits stated: it tells us nothing when the vector field starts with quadratic or higher-order terms [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch07-limit-p1]] (p. 210): Jacobian trace and determinant decide the fixed point's type in Example 7.3.3 [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p1]] (pp. 247–248): classify fixed points by trace and determinant of the Jacobian
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p2]] (p. 284): stability of the closed orbit from the linearized return map
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p1]] (p. 322): trace and determinant of the 2×2 matrix at the origin [as `linear stability analysis`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch10-one-p1]] (p. 357): linearize $\eta_{n+1}=f'(x^*)\eta_n$; multiplier $\lambda$ decides
