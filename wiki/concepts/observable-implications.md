# observable-implications

## Canon spine entry

- **observable implications** (political-science): testable consequences of a theory, ideally many and varied (KKV) (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch06-musical]] (pp. 164–168): the authors test testable consequences of the selection model (post-election growth, variance, vote margin) [as `observable implications`]
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch02-science]] (p. 25): consequences deduced from a model beyond the data it was built on. More, varied and surprising ones are better [as `observable implications`]
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch06-economic-p1]] (pp. 177–179): testable consequences deduced from each theory (Table 6.1)
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch07-cultural-p1]] (p. 239): the missing requirement for ex post explanations
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch14-social-p3]] (p. 653): Duverger's law and hypothesis are called "observational implications" of his theory

- [[grimmer-2022-textasdata/ch15-principles]] (p. 278): testable consequences of a theory that measures should be able to test
