# narrative-contagion-sir

## Canon spine entry

- **narrative contagion (SIR)** (economics-finance): stories spread and fade like epidemics (UG)

### economics-finance

- [[shiller-2019-narrative/ch06-diverse]] (p. 60, 62): stories spread by contact and fade by forgetting; contagion and forgetting rates can be influenced [as `narrative contagion (SIR)`]
