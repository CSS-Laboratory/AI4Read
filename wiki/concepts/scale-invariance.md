# scale-invariance

## Canon spine entry

- **scale invariance** (complex-systems): no characteristic scale, so the system looks statistically similar across scales (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[bak-1987-soc/main]] (p. 382): no characteristic length, hence no characteristic time [as `scale invariance`]
- [[barabasi-1999-scaling/main]] (pp. 3–4): "scale-free" here means connectivity has no characteristic scale, a power-law distribution
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch15-science]] (p. 111): shape of the distribution stays the same when rescaled, "self-similar"
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch17-mystery]] (p. 137): self-similarity at all scales of magnification
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 56): scale-invariant patch-size distributions near a systemic transition; lost in some systems [as `scale invariance`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch11-fractals]] (p. 409): here self-similarity of fractals, exact in the Cantor set and approximate in general
