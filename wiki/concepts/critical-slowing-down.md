# critical-slowing-down

## Canon spine entry

- **critical slowing down** (complex-systems): slower recovery from perturbations near a tipping point, seen as rising autocorrelation and variance (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p3]] (pp. 41, 47): stated to characterize the continuous ($\eta\ge2$) mean-field hierarchy transition, and observed at $\phi_c$
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 53): recovery from small perturbations slows as the dominant eigenvalue nears zero [as `critical slowing down`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p1]] (p. 57): algebraic instead of exponential decay when the linearisation vanishes at $r=0$ of the pitchfork
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p1]] (p. 249): algebraic instead of exponential decay at μ = 0
