# paradigm-normal-science

## Canon spine entry

- **paradigm / normal science** (analytic-philosophy): a shared exemplar-plus-framework that defines legitimate puzzles, and the puzzle-solving it supports (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch05-kuhn]] (pp. 76–77): a whole way of doing science, including exemplars, which guides the choice of puzzles; normal science is the work done within it. Broad sense vs. narrow sense (exemplar) [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch06-kuhn]] (p. 87): shared framework and the orderly puzzle-solving within it; chapter 5 terms assumed [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch07-lakatos]] (p. 102, 112): in this chapter, a Kuhnian field usually has one paradigm; Lakatos and Feyerabend's criticisms of it [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch13-explanation]] (p. 197): Kuhn: paradigms bring their own standards of explanation and of causal relations [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch15-empiricism]] (p. 227): Kuhn-style paradigm-dominated work, which may fit some fields and not others [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch02-route]] (p. 10): Here: an achievement that attracts an enduring group and leaves open problems; normal science is research based on it (p. 10). Kuhn says the paradigm is a concrete model (law, theory, application, instrumentation together), not only a theory [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch03-nature]] (pp. 23–24): Paradigm: an accepted model of work. Kuhn stresses that it is rarely an object of replication but of articulation, and is at first largely a promise. Normal science is the research that fulfils it [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch04-normal]] (pp. 35, 37): normal research carried out within a paradigm; here, work that aims at puzzle solutions, not novelty [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch05-priority]] (p. 43): here, recurrent quasi-standard illustrations of theory in applications, found in textbooks and lab exercises, by which members learn their trade [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch06-anomaly]] (pp. 52, 60–61, 65): the shared framework in which puzzle-solving occurs. Here it also supplies expectations that make anomalies visible, and it restricts the accessible field [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch07-crisis]] (pp. 68, 72, 76): the working framework whose puzzle-solving failures drive crisis; in the chemistry case it was still believed and trusted as a working tool during crisis [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch08-response]] (pp. 79–80): the shared basis of current research practice; normal science solves puzzles whose very existence presupposes the paradigm's validity [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch09-nature]] (pp. 103, 109): paradigm is a shared framework supplying theory, methods and standards of problem and solution; here its normative role is stressed [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch11-invisibility]] (pp. 136–137): normal science rests on the community's current paradigms; textbooks are its pedagogic vehicles [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch12-resolution]] (pp. 144–145): the shared framework that makes research puzzle-solving. Here its testing is possible only by competition with a rival [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch13-progress]] (pp. 162–164): normal science is puzzle-solving within one paradigm; here it explains why progress is obvious and efficient [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/front-preface]] (pdf 8): here only "paradigm" appears: universally recognized achievements giving a community model problems and solutions for a time (pdf 8). "Normal science" is not named in the Preface. [as `paradigm / normal science`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/postscript-1969]] (pp. 175, 182): in the postscript, "paradigm" has two senses: the whole disciplinary matrix, and exemplars. Normal science is puzzle-solving by a community on this shared basis [as `paradigm / normal science`]
