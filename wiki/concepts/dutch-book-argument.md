# dutch-book-argument

## Canon spine entry

- **Dutch book argument** (analytic-philosophy): if your degrees of belief violate probability theory, someone can offer you a set of bets that guarantees you a loss (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch14-bayesianism]] (p. 207): incoherent degrees of belief permit bets with guaranteed loss

### bayesian

- [[gelman-2013-bda3/ch01-probability]] (p. 13): the text calls it the "coherence of bets": the principle says your probabilities should not permit a definite gain by betting against you, and probabilities constructed this way must satisfy the basic axioms. The phrase "Dutch book" does not appear in this unit

### information-theory

- [[jaynes-2003-probability/appa-other]] (p. 655): de Finetti's *coherence* (no sure loss in betting), which Jaynes rejects as a foundation
