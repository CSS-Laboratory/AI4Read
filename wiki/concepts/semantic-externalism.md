# semantic-externalism

## Canon spine entry

- **semantic externalism** (analytic-philosophy): what words mean depends partly on the speaker's physical and social environment, not only on what is in the head (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[lycan-2019-language/ch04-proper]] (pp. 62–63): if the Kripke–Putnam view is correct, meaning depends partly on the external world, so Twin Earth duplicates can mean different things
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch06-use]] (pp. 82–83): Twin Earth meaning differences are used against use theories. Sellars' environment-involving rules are offered as a reply [as `semantic externalism`]
- [[putnam-1975-meaning/main]] (pp. 144–146, 164): extension depends on society and environment, not only on the head [as `semantic externalism`]
