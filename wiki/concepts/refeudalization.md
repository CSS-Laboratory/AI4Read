# refeudalization

## Canon spine entry

- **Refeudalization** (communication-theory): the public sphere's collapse into staged publicity by organized interests and commercial media (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[habermas-1989-publicsphere/ch05-social]] (p. 158): societal powers taking on public functions (p. 142); also the new "neighborhood" closeness in which private and public cannot be clearly distinguished
- [[habermas-1989-publicsphere/ch06-transformation-p1]] (p. 195, p. 200): the public sphere takes on feudal traits: pomp, aura of personal prestige and acclamation replace critical debate
- [[habermas-1989-publicsphere/ch06-transformation-p2]] (p. 232): public and private spheres interlock: political authorities take over social functions, societal powers take over political ones (p. 231); the process reaches into the political public sphere, where organizations seek compromises while staging publicity for a mediatized public
