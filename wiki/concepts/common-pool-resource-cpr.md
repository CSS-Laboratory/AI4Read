# common-pool-resource-cpr

## Canon spine entry

- **common-pool resource (CPR)** (political-science): a resource from which exclusion is hard but use is rival (fisheries, pastures, irrigation water) (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch02-institutional]] (p. 30): resource system large enough that excluding beneficiaries is costly but not impossible; units are subtractable
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch03-analyzing-p2]] (p. 88): shared resource system governed by its users. Here, irrigation systems and mountain commons
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch04-analyzing]] (p. 108): a groundwater basin without limits on who may withdraw is an open-access CPR [as `common-pool resource (CPR)`]
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch05-analyzing]] (p. 143): shared resource (fishery, groundwater, irrigation water, forest) where users' rules matter
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch06-framework]] (p. 182): a resource whose units are scarce and shared. The chapter studies small-scale CPRs with up to about 15,000 users
- [[shepsle-2010-analyzing/ch10-public-p2]] (p. 335): a commons: owned by everyone, so the responsibility of no one [as `common-pool resource (CPR)`]
