# dispersed-tacit-knowledge

## Canon spine entry

- **dispersed / tacit knowledge** (economics-finance): economically relevant knowledge is local and unwritten, so no planner can gather it (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[hayek-1945-knowledge/main]] (§ III): Hayek's emphasis: knowledge of particular circumstances of time and place, unorganised, held by individuals, never given in total to anyone. Hayek calls it "unorganized", not "tacit"
