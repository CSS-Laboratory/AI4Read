# language-game

## Canon spine entry

- **language-game** (analytic-philosophy): a rule-governed activity in which words get their point, such as giving orders, reporting or joking (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch08-challenge]] (p. 131): linguistic habits within a form of life; their social use is all there is to meaning
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch06-use]] (p. 81): a rule-governed social practice in which words are moves, such as greeting or wedding talk
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch01-augustine]] (p. 8e): a primitive language, a naming or repeating exercise, or "the whole" of language plus the activities it is woven into
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch02-family]] (§71, p. 38e; §130, p. 56e): simple language-games are offered as "objects of comparison", not as first approximations for a future regimentation of language; §71 also speaks of the language-game with the word "game"
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch03-understanding]] (§179, pp. 78e–79e): the setting in which *Now I can go on* is used and learnt, within a family of language-games
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch04-private]] (pp. 99, 106): an activity in which words are used; §290 says the game with a sensation-word does not end with the same expression but begins with it, and §261 says a sound is an expression only inside a particular game
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch05-thinking]] (§363, p. 121e): the practice (e.g. communicating) in which words get a point; Wittgenstein asks what the language-game of communicating is
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch06-intentionality]] (§486, p. 144e): an activity of using words; named when asking what justification is
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch07-negation]] (§§630, 654–656, pp. 171e, 175e): the practice (e.g. reporting a past intention, predicting from a decision) taken as primary, not explained by experiences
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch08-ppf]] (p. 188e): the practice in which an expression has its use, such as reporting, dreams, or *I believe*
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch09-ppf]] (p. 210e): the primitive language-game children are taught needs no justification
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch10-ppf]] (§273, p. 227e): a context of use; the kind of certainty is the kind of language-game (§332, p. 236e), and "meaning it" in the word-experiencing game takes its expression over from another language-game
