# preference-relation

## Canon spine entry

- **preference relation** (decision-game-theory): a complete, transitive ranking of options; the primitive of rational choice (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[kahneman-1979-prospect/main]] (p. 289): appendix prints the preference relation as "connected, symmetric and transitive" [as `preference relation`; work fields: decision-game-theory, economics-finance]
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch27-endowment]] (pdf 281–282): indifference curves list equally desirable combinations; the author says they wrongly ignore the reference point
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch01-introduction]] (p. 4): for any pair she knows which is preferred or that she is indifferent, and preferences are transitive [as `preference relation`]
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch02-nash-p1]] (p. 12): a player's ranking of action profiles. It is often represented by payoffs that have only ordinal meaning
