# mentalizing-network

## Canon spine entry

- **mentalizing network** (theory-of-mind): brain regions reliably engaged by mental-state reasoning: TPJ, mPFC, precuneus, temporal poles (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[apperly-2009-two-systems/main-p2]] (p. 43): regions including medial prefrontal cortex, superior temporal sulcus and temporo-parietal junction
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch04-evidence]] (pp. 60–63): the set of regions (mPFC, TPJ, temporal poles, precuneus) repeatedly active in mindreading tasks; here the claim is that it is not specific to mindreading
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch07-elaborating]] (p. 168): several regions (temporal poles, mPFC, lateral PFC, TPJ) are suggested to form a network for the flexible construction of situation models in high-level mindreading
- [[heyes-2014-cultural-evolution/main]] (p. 1): medial prefrontal cortex, temporo-parietal junction, precuneus [as `mentalizing network`]
- [[saxe-2003-tpj/main]] (pp. 3, 5): other regions found for ToM > control: aSTS, precuneus, medial prefrontal [as `mentalizing network`]
