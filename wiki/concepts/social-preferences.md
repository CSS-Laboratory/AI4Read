# social-preferences

## Canon spine entry

- **social preferences** (decision-game-theory): caring about others' payoffs (fairness, reciprocity), as shown in ultimatum and dictator data (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[axelrod-1984-cooperation/ch07-promote]] (p. 135): here "altruism": one's utility rises with another's welfare
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch02-games]] (pp. 63, 66): fairness and altruism in players' objectives, shown by ultimatum and dictator play [as `social preferences`]
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch03-prisoners]] (p. 90): pleasure from punishing cheaters at personal cost [as `social preferences`]
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch04-beautiful]] (p. 130): other-regarding preferences, used to explain ultimatum and dilemma experiments
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch06-extensive]] (pp. 181–182): equity-conscious payoffs $x_i-\beta_i|x_1-x_2|$ as one explanation for ultimatum-game data
