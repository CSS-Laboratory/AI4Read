# price-as-signal

## Canon spine entry

- **price as signal** (economics-finance): prices aggregate and transmit private information about scarcity and value (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[grossman-1980-impossibility/main]] (p. 393): the price transmits informed traders' information to the uninformed, but only partially
- [[hayek-1945-knowledge/main]] (§§ V–VI): prices condense information on relative scarcity and pass it, in abbreviated form, only to those concerned
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch11-marginal]] (§ III): loosely related: existing-asset prices adjust at once to expectations [as `price as signal`; weak: not really used in this unit]
- [[stiglitz-2002-paradigm/main-p3]] (p. 522): in the competitive paradigm prices convey all relevant information; Stiglitz uses this to frame the transparency debate [as `price as signal`]
