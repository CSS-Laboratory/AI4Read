# mindblindness

## Canon spine entry

- **mindblindness** (theory-of-mind): the hypothesis that autism involves a specific deficit in attributing mental states (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch02-evidence]] (p. 32): claims that autism is due to a lack of mindreading concepts; the author urges caution about them
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch07-elaborating]] (pp. 166–167): the autism deficit is doubted as a general explanation. A low-level implicit false-belief deficit is reported
- [[baron-cohen-1985-autism/main]] (p. 43): related to the hypothesis of a specific deficit in attributing mental states in autism; the paper does not use the term, and its claim is a deficit in representing mental states, specific to autism [weak: not really used in this unit]
- [[heyes-2014-cultural-evolution/main]] (p. 1): autism-associated specific mind-reading impairment (as "ASCs")
- [[onishi-2005-infant-false-belief/main]]: autism described as deficient theory of mind (para. 15)
