# rational-ignorance

## Canon spine entry

- **rational ignorance** (political-science): staying uninformed is individually rational when information costs more than its expected value to a single voter (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch02-elusive]] (p. 30): Downs: a single vote matters so little that many rational voters buy no political information [as `rational ignorance`]
- [[achen-2016-realists/ch11-groups]] (p. 324): appear only in Zaller's summary of the Downsian optimistic view
- [[downs-1957-democracy/main]] (pp. 146–147, 149): staying uninformed because the information costs more than its expected return to one voter [as `rational ignorance`]
- [[lupia-1998-dilemma/ch02-people]] (p. fn. 7, p. 20): the authors accept that the costs of acquiring information often outweigh its benefits but add that the benefit may itself be zero or negative
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch02-information]] (p. 16): Downs's phrase for the low political information of most Americans, who still differ greatly in degree [as `rational ignorance`]
