# family-resemblance

## Canon spine entry

- **family resemblance** (analytic-philosophy): members of a concept such as "game" share overlapping similarities, not one common essence (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[kripke-1980-naming/ch01-lecture-p1]] (p. 31): Wittgenstein's idea used for the family of descriptions behind a name [as `family resemblance`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch02-route]] (p. 14): Used loosely: Kuhn says the electrical theories had no more than a family resemblance (p. 14). He does not define the phrase here. [as `family resemblance`]
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch05-priority]] (p. 45): Wittgenstein's idea that class members are linked by overlapping resemblances and not by a common essence; Kuhn extends it to research problems as a possibility [as `family resemblance`]
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch02-family]] (§§66–67, pp. 36e–37e): a concept's members linked by overlapping, criss-crossing similarities, with no one feature common to all [as `family resemblance`]
- [[wittgenstein-1953-investigations/ch03-understanding]] (§164, p. 72e): *derive* and *read* apply to families of cases with changing criteria; W. uses the word *family* for the cases, not the term *resemblance* [as `family resemblance`]
