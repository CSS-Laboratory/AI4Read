# notional-world

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch05-beyond-p1]] (pp. 152–155, 158–159): a theorist's fictional world, perhaps a set of possible worlds, used to characterize narrow states; consists of things represented
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch05-beyond-p2]] (pp. 182, 204, 210): the world of the subject's notional attitudes, which may contain non-existent items (Santa). It lets psychological states be characterised indirectly through descriptions of a "world" [new]
