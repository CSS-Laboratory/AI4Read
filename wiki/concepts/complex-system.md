# complex-system

## Canon spine entry

- **complex system** (complex-systems): many interacting parts whose collective behavior is not obvious from the parts alone (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch01-complexity]] (pdf 31): here: a system of many simple, uncontrolled components giving collective behavior, information processing and adaptation [as `complex system`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch02-dynamics]] (pdf 33, 62): a system whose hallmark is changing, hard-to-predict macroscopic behaviour; dynamical systems theory studies such behaviour emerging from many interacting components [as `complex system`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch10-cellular]] (pdf 186): many simple components, no central controller; CAs idealize this
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch18-evolution]] (p. 150): here, living systems whose complexity is largely due to networks of interacting genes [as `complex system`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch19-past]] (p. 174): in general system theory, a "system" is a collection of interacting elements producing system-wide behavior; Mitchell notes this is very general [as `complex system`]

- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p1]] (p. 309): here, a simple deterministic 3D system (Lorenz, waterwheel) with very erratic dynamics [as `complex system`]
