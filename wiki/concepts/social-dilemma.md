# social-dilemma

## Canon spine entry

- **social dilemma** (economics-finance): individually rational choices produce a collectively worse outcome (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[keynes-1936-general/ch09-propensity]] (§ II, para. 4): here, a later reading and not Keynes's wording: a more thrifty community sees its income fall when interest rises relative to the marginal efficiency of capital
- [[thaler-2015-misbehaving/ch15-fairness]] (pp. 105–107): individually selfish choices make the group worse off; seen in the Prisoner's Dilemma and Public Goods Game [as `social dilemma`]
- [[thaler-2015-misbehaving/ch30-game]] (p. 257): Golden Balls final as a version of the Prisoner's Dilemma; in a game played once, the selfish rational strategy is for both to defect
