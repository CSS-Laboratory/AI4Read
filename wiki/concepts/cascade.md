# cascade

## Canon spine entry

- **cascade** (complex-systems): a chain reaction of adoptions or failures spreading through a population (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[bak-1987-soc/main]] (p. 381): the "domino" effect that spreads a perturbation through minimally stable sites
- [[granovetter-1978-threshold/main]] (p. 1424): "bandwagon" or "domino" chain in which each adopter activates the next threshold
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch16-applying]] (p. 126): here "cascading failure": failure passes load on to other nodes
