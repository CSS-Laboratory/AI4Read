# effective-demand

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[keynes-1936-general/ch03-principle]]: value of $D$ where aggregate demand meets aggregate supply; fixes employment (para. 4) [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch06-definition]] (§ I, para. 8): aggregate income entrepreneurs expect from the employment they choose to give [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch07-meaning]] (§III, para. 2): entrepreneurs' estimate of demand, based on expected proceeds from consumption and investment; Keynes says employment is determined by these estimates [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch16-sundry]] (§I ¶2): expected demand that gives entrepreneurs reason to employ labour. Keynes says expected consumption is the only reason for employment [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch18-general]] (§ I): demand level, in wage-units, that determines the employment given by the aggregate supply function [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch19-changes]] (§ II, "first question"): expected consumption plus expected investment; employment is uniquely tied to it in wage-units [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch20-employment]] (§ I, para. 1): expected spending directed to output, measured in wage-units or money [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch21-theory]]: expected, gross income that sets production moving (§ IV(1)) [new]
