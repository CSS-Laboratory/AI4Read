# subgame-perfect-equilibrium

## Canon spine entry

- **subgame-perfect equilibrium** (decision-game-theory): Selten's refinement requiring optimal play in every subgame, ruling out non-credible threats (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch06-strategic]] (p. 173): Selten's formal concept of credibility, mentioned only
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch07-making]] (p. 219): in the epilogue, Selten's idea of strategies optimal even if play takes an unexpected turn [as `subgame-perfect equilibrium`]
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch14-case]] (p. 395): the equilibrium concept the authors name for Solomon's game, in which the true mother gets the child [as `subgame-perfect equilibrium`]
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch05-extensive]] (p. 164): a strategy profile that is optimal for every player after every history where she moves; equivalently, induces a Nash equilibrium in every subgame [as `subgame-perfect equilibrium`]
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch06-extensive]] (pp. 180, 185, 194): a strategy profile that is optimal in every subgame; here found by backward induction in finite-horizon games
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch07-extensive]] (p. 205): profile with no profitable deviation in any subgame; "turn to move" means $i\in P(h)$ [as `subgame-perfect equilibrium`]
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch14-repeated]] (p. 404): strategies optimal after every history, including off-path ones
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch15-repeated]] (p. 416): checked here with the one-deviation property: a profile is subgame perfect if and only if it satisfies it
