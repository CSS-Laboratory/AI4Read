# saddle-node-fold-bifurcation

## Canon spine entry

- **saddle-node (fold) bifurcation** (complex-systems): two fixed points collide and vanish, the mathematics of most tipping points (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 53, Box 1 p. 54): equilibrium curve folded backwards; the unstable middle branch marks the basin border; dominant eigenvalue becomes zero at the fold points F1, F2 [as `saddle-node (fold) bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p1]] (pp. 46–48): two fixed points collide and vanish. Normal form $\dot x=r\pm x^2$. Also called fold, turning-point or blue-sky bifurcation
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p2]] (p. 71, p. 77): two fixed points collide and vanish where a line is tangent to a curve; here along $h=\pm h_c(r)$ and the budworm curves
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch04-flows]] (p. 99): two fixed points collide at $a=\omega$ (or $\gamma=1$, $\mu=1$, $I=I_c$) and vanish [as `saddle-node (fold) bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p3]] (p. 298): fixed points collide and vanish; also of cycles, in the averaged forced Duffing system
