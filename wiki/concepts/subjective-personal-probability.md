# subjective-personal-probability

## Canon spine entry

- **Subjective (personal) probability** (bayesian): probability as a coherent degree of belief of an agent, measurable through betting (de Finetti) (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### bayesian

- [[efron-1986-bayesian/main]] (p. 3): de Finetti–Savage subjective Bayes, which has the legitimate claim to philosophic coherency but, per Efron, has failed to make much of a dent in scientific statistical practice
- [[mcgrayne-2011-theory/ch11-business]] (pp. 145, 148): belief expressed in bets and judgment; Raiffa's .92 statement
