# screening

## Canon spine entry

- **screening** (economics-finance): the uninformed party designs a menu of options so that types reveal themselves (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[akerlof-1970-lemons/main]] (pp. 493–494): insurers and employers sort applicants themselves (insurers "screen"), as a response to hidden quality
- [[stiglitz-2002-paradigm/main-p1]] (pp. 478, 492): an uninformed party (or an exam) sorts types; here also the informed party's costly credential, e.g. education
- [[stiglitz-2002-paradigm/main-p2]] (p. 498): the uninformed side designs choices or tests to sort types

### decision-game-theory

- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch08-interpreting]] (pp. 226–227): less-informed player sets up a choice so that each type's action reveals its type [new]
