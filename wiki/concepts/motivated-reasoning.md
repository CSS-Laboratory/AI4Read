## Canon spine entry

- **motivated reasoning / motivated skepticism** (political-science): processing evidence to reach a desired conclusion: accepting congenial claims easily and scrutinizing uncongenial ones (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch10-it]] (pp. 268–269, 293–294): here "rationalization": constructing reasons that bolster existing commitments
- [[bail-2018-opposingviews/main]] (§ "Introduction"): the cited mechanism for backfire: counterarguing messages that conflict with one's own attitudes [alias `motivated-reasoning-motivated-skepticism`]
- [[chong-2007-framing/main]] (p. 120): evaluating information to support preconceptions and devalue contrary evidence (Lodge & Taber); linked to strong attitudes [work fields: political-science, communication-theory]
- [[kahan-2013-motivatedreasoning/main]] (p. 408): fitting assessments of information to a goal other than accuracy; here, protecting group standing (p. 408). Canon: motivated reasoning / motivated skepticism.

### communication-theory

- [[lewandowsky-2012-misinformation/main]] (p. 119): accepting supporting arguments uncritically and attacking opposing ones [alias `motivated-skepticism`; new]
