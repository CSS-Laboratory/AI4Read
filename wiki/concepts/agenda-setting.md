# agenda-setting

## Canon spine entry

- **Agenda setting** (communication-theory): media salience of issues transfers to public salience: "what to think about" (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[lazer-2018-fakenews/main]] (p. 8): media typically cannot change attitudes but are far more successful at changing what we think about
- [[mccombs-1972-agendasetting/main]] (pp. 177, 184): media emphasis shapes the salience of issues for the public; a hypothesis here, not proved
- [[rogers-2003-diffusion/ch04-generation]] (pdf 190): here, how a social problem rises to high priority, e.g. auto safety
- [[rogers-2003-diffusion/ch10-innovation]] (pdf 515): here used inside organizations: how problems are prioritized for attention.; the text cites Dearing and Rogers (1996)
