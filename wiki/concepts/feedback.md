# feedback

## Canon spine entry

- **Feedback** (communication-theory): output fed back as input to steer a system toward a goal; the core of cybernetics (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### control-theory

- [[ashby-1956-cybernetics/ch04-machine]] (p. 54): circularity of action: each of two parts affects the other (p. 53); Ashby says the exact definition is unimportant
- [[ashby-1956-cybernetics/ch06-black]] (p. 87): here the Box ↔ Experimenter coupling forms a system with feedback
- [[ashby-1956-cybernetics/ch08-transmission]] (p. 157): in a determinate system, feedback between T and U gives no regenerative increase in variety
- [[ashby-1956-cybernetics/ch12-error]] (p. 223): information passing from E back to R
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch01-introduction]] (pp. 1-1, 1-13): two or more dynamical systems coupled so each influences the other; correction based on desired vs actual performance
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch02-feedback]] (p. 2-1): here: a loop in which the controller input is $e=r-y$, the difference between reference and process output
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch03-system-p2]] (p. 3-25): the fly's visual motion measurement steers the wings, closing the loop
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch04-examples]] (p. 4-7, p. 4-10): measured output used to adjust input; here also built into mechanics (bicycle fork) and circuits
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch05-dynamic]] (p. 5-1): here $u=\alpha(x)$, the system regulating itself
- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch12-frequency]] (p. 12-2): $C$ acts on the measured output $y$, with $-1$ in the loop
- [[kalman-1960-filtering/main]] (p. 42): the optimal filter is a feedback system, from inspection of Fig. 3
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/app-untitled]] (p. 187): a closed chain from a stock, via decisions, back to a flow changing that stock
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/ch00-introduction]] (p. 5): named here as the basic operating unit of a system; no formal definition in this unit
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/ch01-basics]] (pp. 25, 27): a closed chain of causation from a stock, through level-dependent decisions or rules, back to a flow into or out of that stock
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/ch04-systems]] (p. 89): loops of stocks and flows that generate behavior; the chapter stresses that structure drives behavior
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/ch05-system]] (p. 117): missing or weak feedback is the cause of the commons trap
- [[meadows-2008-thinking/ch07-living]] (p. 170): information about results used to adjust later decisions; with nonlinearity and self-responsible systems, one of the three ideas her worldview rests on
- [[sutton-2018-rl/ch15-neuroscience-p2]] (p. 403): in Klopf's view, feedback loops inside and outside the animal shape a neuron's trace
- [[taylor-1993-discretion/main]] (p. 198): author's "feedback rules": instrument settings respond to measured inflation, unemployment or output
- [[welch-2004-kalmanintro/main]] (p. 4): the author says the filter uses "a form of feedback control": measurements correct the estimate
- [[wlezien-1995-thermostat/main]] (p. 981): policy outputs "feed back" on public inputs (Easton's model)

### complex-systems
