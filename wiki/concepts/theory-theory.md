# theory-theory

## Canon spine entry

- **theory-theory** (theory-of-mind): ToM is a tacit, causal-explanatory theory of mind, learned or revised like a scientific theory (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[apperly-2009-two-systems/main-p1]] (p. 4): roughly Gopnik & Meltzoff's alternative as described: general reasoning plus learned knowledge about beliefs
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch01-introduction]] (p. 5): mindreading depends on having mental-state concepts and principles about their interactions
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch04-evidence]] (p. 85): mentioned only in note 11: the mPFC similarity findings do not, in Apperly's view, discriminate them
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch06-cognitive]] (pp. 122–123): discussed critically: no formal rule system for mindreading has been articulated, so the grammar analogy fails
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch07-elaborating]] (p. 176): mindreading by concepts and principles about mental states. Apperly's account treats high-level mindreading as another kind of reasoning problem, so it is partly compatible
- [[heyes-2014-cultural-evolution/main]] (p. 4): children learn explicit theories by observation and hypothesis testing. Here the novice's ideas come mainly from instruction rather than from self-generated theories
- [[saxe-2003-tpj/main]] (p. 1): the narrower claim that mental knowledge is structured like a scientific theory; mentioned in a footnote, not used
