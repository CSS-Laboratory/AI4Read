# identity-protective-cognition

## Canon spine entry

- **identity-protective cognition** (political-science): reasoning that protects one's standing in a valued group, which can be stronger among the more cognitively skilled (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[kahan-2013-motivatedreasoning/main]] (pp. 408–409): "identity self-defense": unconsciously resisting facts contrary to one's group's dominant belief. Kahan adds that it often serves individual interest
