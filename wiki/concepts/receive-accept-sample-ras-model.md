# receive-accept-sample-ras-model

## Canon spine entry

- **Receive–Accept–Sample (RAS) model** (political-science): opinion as a sample of considerations that the person has received and accepted, weighted by political awareness and predispositions (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch04-coming]] (p. 58): axioms A1–A4: reception, resistance, accessibility and response; treated as an information-processing model
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch06-mainstream]] (p. 98): the model generating both effects; the chapter derives from it via A1 and A2
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch07-basic]] (p. 118): opinion as sample of received and accepted considerations. Here, attitude change means a permanent change in response probabilities
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch08-tests]] (pp. 151, 154): here the "reception–acceptance" part of RAS: opinion change needs reception of a message and then acceptance; both depend on awareness
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch10-information-p2]] (p. 248): opinion formed from messages received, then accepted or resisted. Extended here to Senate, presidential and primary data
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch11-evaluating-p1]] (pp. 265, 273): Zaller's model: reception, acceptance, accessibility, averaging response; here also a "generic diffusion model"
