# deterministic-chaos

## Canon spine entry

- **deterministic chaos** (complex-systems): bounded, aperiodic behavior from deterministic rules with sensitive dependence on initial conditions (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[lorenz-1963-nonperiodic/main]] (p. 133): bounded, nonperiodic deterministic solutions that are unstable. Lorenz does not use the word "chaos" [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch02-dynamics]] (pdf 39, 54): sensitive dependence on initial conditions in a deterministic system, which looks random [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch18-evolution]] (p. 160): RBN behaviour that never settles, with sensitive dependence on the initial state [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch01-overview]] (pp. 2–3): deterministic, aperiodic behaviour with sensitive dependence on initial conditions, making long-term prediction impossible [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch07-limit-p1]] (p. 212): said to be impossible in the phase plane [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p1]] (p. 331): aperiodic, deterministic, sensitive to initial conditions [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p2]] (p. 340): long-term aperiodic behaviour with sensitive dependence [as `deterministic chaos`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch10-one-p1]] (p. 373): aperiodic, sensitive dependence; here tested by $\lambda>0$
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch12-strange]] (p. 432): bounded aperiodic behaviour; here shown to come from deterministic maps or flows [as `deterministic chaos`]
