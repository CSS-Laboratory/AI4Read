# independence-of-irrelevant-alternatives-iia

## Canon spine entry

- **independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA)** (political-science): the social ranking of A versus B should depend only on individuals' rankings of A versus B (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[arrow-1951-socialchoice/ch04-iv]] (p. 40): Arrow's Condition 3 as used here: x–y choice should not depend on orderings of alternatives outside $[x,y]$ (p. 40) (canon link; name not used in this unit) [as `independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA)`]
- [[arrow-1951-socialchoice/ch05-v]] (pp. 47, 49): Condition 3: the social choice from $S$ depends only on individual orderings within $S$ [as `independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA)`]
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch11-problems-p1]] (pp. 424, 424 fn. 12): in this chapter the Borda count's failure: adding an "irrelevant" alternative changes the group ranking of the others. Fn. 12 notes the technical IIA is about the *ranking* of such an alternative
