# age-4-transition

## Canon spine entry

- **age-4 transition** (theory-of-mind): the robust shift from failing to passing explicit false-belief tasks around age 4 (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch02-evidence]] (p. 12): passing probability changes faster between 3 and 4 than between 4 and 5 in the meta-analysis
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch03-evidence]] (p. 36): 3–4-year transition on explicit false-belief tasks
- [[onishi-2005-infant-false-belief/main]]: View A's claimed shift at about age 4; the authors question it (paras. 2, 14)
- [[wimmer-1983-beliefs-about-beliefs/main]] (p. 124): abrupt improvement from age 3–4 to 4–5 on belief and knowledge tasks
