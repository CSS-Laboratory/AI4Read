# selective-exposure-media-choice

## Canon spine entry

- **selective exposure / media choice** (political-science): people choose media that match their tastes or politics, so the high-choice environment widens gaps between groups (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[bail-2018-opposingviews/main]] (§ "Introduction"): people's choice of information that fits their views; the paper tries to *disrupt* it experimentally
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch07-basic]] (p. 139–140): Zaller's "selective reception". He judges that, on the available evidence, it apparently does not occur broadly enough to affect the diffusion of major ideas
