# encoding-decoding

## Canon spine entry

- **Encoding/decoding** (communication-theory): meaning is produced at encoding and re-produced at decoding; it can be dominant, negotiated or oppositional (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[hall-1973-encoding/main]] (pp. 3–4): a programme is encoded as a meaningful discourse and then decoded; meanings I and II may not be the same
