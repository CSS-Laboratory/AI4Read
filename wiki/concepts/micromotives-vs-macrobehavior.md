# micromotives-vs-macrobehavior

## Canon spine entry

- **micromotives vs. macrobehavior** (complex-systems): individual intentions can produce collective outcomes nobody wants (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[granovetter-1978-threshold/main]] (pp. 1421, 1422): collective outcomes can be intuitively inconsistent with individual intentions. The paper cites Schelling as the source of thresholds but does not use this phrase
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p1]] (pp. 145–146): individual choices can produce collective outcomes that no one intended and that need not reflect what anyone wants
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p2]] (pp. 13, 36): collective patterns (density, dead spaces, tipping) that arise from individual decision rules; tipping analysis must relate collective results to individual motives [as `micromotives vs. macrobehavior`]
