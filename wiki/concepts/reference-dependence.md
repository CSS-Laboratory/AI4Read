# reference-dependence

## Canon spine entry

- **reference dependence** (decision-game-theory): outcomes are coded as gains or losses relative to a reference point, not as final wealth (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[kahneman-1979-prospect/main]] (pp. 274, 286): outcomes coded as gains or losses against a reference point, usually the status quo, but possibly an expectation or aspiration level [as `reference dependence`]
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/appb-choices]] (pdf 433): value attaches to changes from a reference point, not to total wealth [as `reference dependence`]
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch25-bernoulli]] (pdf 266): outcomes are judged as changes from a reference point, not as final wealth [as `reference dependence`]
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch26-prospect]] (pdf 272–274): outcomes are coded as gains or losses from a reference point (status quo, expectation or perhaps entitlement), not as final wealth
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch27-endowment]] (pdf 282): outcomes are judged as gains or losses relative to the current position or status quo
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch28-bad]] (pdf 294): good vs. bad is judged relative to a status quo, goal, par, or existing price
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch29-fourfold]] (pdf 308): people attach values to gains and losses rather than to wealth [as `reference dependence`]
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch34-frames]] (pdf 360–361): outcomes judged relative to a reference point, such as keeping vs losing, or a tax default
- [[kahneman-2011-thinking/ch38-thinking]] (pdf 397): only touched on: prospect theory represents a state by the transition to it [as `reference dependence`]
