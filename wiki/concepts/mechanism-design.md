# mechanism-design

## Canon spine entry

- **mechanism design** (decision-game-theory): choosing game rules so that self-interested play yields a target outcome (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch07-making]] (pp. 220–221): designing a game or contract so that others reveal information or act as desired [as `mechanism design`]
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch10-auctions]] (p. 284): the authors' stated goal is to design a game so that players need not be strategic; the Vickrey auction is their example [as `mechanism design`]
- [[myerson-2008-mechanism/main]] (pp. 321–322): choosing rules (a mapping from reports to outcomes) so that self-interested behaviour yields a target outcome [as `mechanism design`]
