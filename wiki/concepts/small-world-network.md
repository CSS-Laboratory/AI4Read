# small-world-network

## Canon spine entry

- **small-world network** (complex-systems): high clustering combined with short average paths (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[barabasi-1999-scaling/main]] (p. 4): WS model: lattice with random rewiring, shorter distances, but P(k) still peaked around z
- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p1]] (p. 6): high clustering, short paths (Watts–Strogatz)
- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p2]] (pp. 26, 32): shortcuts give mean-field-like NG and Axelrod behavior [as `small-world network`]
- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p3]] (p. 46): rumor transition at finite $p_c$
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch15-science]] (p. 105): few long links plus short average path length relative to size; usually high clustering
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch16-applying]] (pp. 116, 121): high clustering with short average paths; reported in brains, food webs
