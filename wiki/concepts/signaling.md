# signaling

## Canon spine entry

- **signaling** (decision-game-theory): informed players take costly actions that reveal private information (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch07-making]] (p. 220): Spence's strategy for coping with information asymmetry
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch08-interpreting]] (pp. 226, 229): informed player takes an action to credibly reveal private information; credible only if optimal if, but only if, the information holds
- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch11-bargaining]] (p. 318): a strike or taking risk proves low waiting costs, at a cost to efficiency
- [[schelling-1960-conflict/ch04-toward]] (pp. 123, 140–141): moves can convey information and commitment that cheap talk often lacks

### economics-finance

- [[akerlof-1970-lemons/main]] (pp. 499–500): guarantees, brand names, licences and diplomas as ways to reveal or certify quality (pp. 499–500); Akerlof does not model the cost of signals
- [[stiglitz-2002-paradigm/main-p2]] (p. 498): the informed side takes the initiative to show he is a better risk, employee or borrower
