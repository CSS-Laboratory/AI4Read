# language-of-thought

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch02-true]] (p. 34): hypothesis that the structural form of internal states copies the logical form of the propositions believed; Dennett thinks some version will prove correct, but not that it is obvious [new]
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch04-making]] (pp. 114–115): hypothesis that sentences in an innate "Mentalese" lie behind belief; Dennett asks for independent support for sentence-like contents [as `language of thought`; new]
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch05-beyond-p1]] (p. 137): Mentalese sentences; sententialism: believers share beliefs iff they share tokens of sentences of a language of thought
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch06-styles]] (pp. 214, 227): Fodor's thesis that thinking is computation over sentence-like internal representations [new]
