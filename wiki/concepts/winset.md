# winset

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[clark-2017-principles/ch11-problems-p2]] (p. 454): set of alternatives that beat the status quo by majority; here the overlap of indifference circles
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch15-institutional-p3]] (pp. 721–722): set of alternatives that beat the status quo. Under veto players, the intersection of all indifference circles [new]
- [[shepsle-2010-analyzing/ch05-spatial-p1]] (p. 95): $W(y)$, union over majority coalitions of the common intersection of their $P_i(y)$ [new]
- [[shepsle-2010-analyzing/ch05-spatial-p2]] (pp. 126, 138, 141): $W(x)$: the points a majority prefers to $x$. In one dimension, $P_m(x^0)$ is the set of points the median (hence a majority) prefers to $x^0$ [new]
- [[shepsle-2010-analyzing/ch05-spatial-p3]] (p. 153): term used in Problem 6 ("empty winsets") with no definition on these pages [new]
