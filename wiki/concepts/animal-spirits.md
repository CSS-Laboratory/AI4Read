# animal-spirits

## Canon spine entry

- **animal spirits** (economics-finance): spontaneous optimism or pessimism that drives investment beyond calculation (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[keynes-1936-general/ch12-state]] (§ VII): spontaneous urge to action that probably drives most positive decisions, rather than weighted-average calculation [as `animal spirits`]
- [[shiller-2019-narrative/ch11-frugality]] (p. 138): Keynes's idea that depressions are caused by declines in "animal spirits." Shiller links Chryold's talk of "spirits" to it [as `animal spirits`]
- [[shiller-2019-narrative/front-preface]] (p. xvi): the idea that the propensity to invest is governed by high or low "animal spirits" among businessmen. Here Kristol attributes it to Keynes and rejects it
- [[thaler-2015-misbehaving/ch21-beauty]] (p. 163): Keynes's emotions that influence investment decisions [as `animal spirits`]
- [[thaler-2015-misbehaving/ch24-price]] (pp. 186–187): Keynes's term, used by Shiller and Akerlof for whimsical shifts in consumer and investor attitudes [as `animal spirits`]
- [[thaler-2015-misbehaving/ch25-battle]] (p. 196): investor sentiment is called an example of them [as `animal spirits`]
