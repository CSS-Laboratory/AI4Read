# bayesian-confirmation

## Canon spine entry

- **Bayesian confirmation** (analytic-philosophy): E confirms H iff P(H|E) > P(H); belief revised by conditionalization (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[godfreysmith-2003-theory/ch14-bayesianism]] (p. 204): e confirms h if $P(h|e)>P(h)$; belief is updated by Bayes's theorem
- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch12-resolution]] (pp. 145–146): "probabilistic verification theories" that ask a theory's probability given the evidence and compare theories; Kuhn objects that in their usual forms they rely on a neutral language [as `Bayesian confirmation`]
- [[popper-1959-logic/appix-untitled]] (pp. 404, 407): the criterion that $y$ supports $x$ iff $p(x,y)>p(x)$, formula (1): the obvious criterion of support, used as assumption (a) of Popper's refutation but failing for universal theories with $p(x)=0$
- [[popper-1959-logic/appvii-untitled-2]] (p. 375, 380): nearest spine id: the probabilistic theory of induction (inductive probability), which Popper calls untenable (p. 374); he holds that $p(a,b)=0$ for universal laws on any finite evidence $b$ (p. 375, 380). The text does not use the word Bayesian
