# preference-falsification

## Canon spine entry

- **preference falsification** (political-science): publicly stating a preference different from one's private one under social pressure (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[clark-2017-principles/ch07-cultural-p1]] (p. 224): hiding true preferences in public for fear of punishment; limits surveys in dictatorships
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch08-democratic-p1]] (p. 283): hiding one's true preference in public, here opposition to the regime
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch08-democratic-p2]] (pp. 290, 301): hiding private preferences in public. Here it helps explain why elites are likely to be uncertain about opposition strength
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch08-democratic-p3]] (pp. 305–306): people hide dislike of the dictatorship and support it publicly [as `preference falsification`]
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch10-varieties-p1]] (p. 364): publicly hiding true preferences. Underlies the dictator's dilemma
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch10-varieties-p2]] (p. 383): may explain why leaders misjudge opposition strength [as `preference falsification`]
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch13-elections-p3]] (p. 592): hiding opposition in dictatorships; here it distorted PUWP's survey data
