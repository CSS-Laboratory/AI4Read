# condorcet-jury-theorem

## Canon spine entry

- **Condorcet jury theorem** (political-science): if voters are independent and each more likely right than wrong, majority accuracy goes to 1 as the group grows (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch02-elusive]] (p. 40): "miracle of aggregation": majority accuracy despite weak individuals, if errors are independent [as `Condorcet jury theorem`]
- [[lupia-1998-dilemma/ch03-people]] (p. 62): mentioned as assuming people believe everything they hear. The authors say its relevance to politics is tenuous
