# exit-voice-loyalty

## Canon spine entry

- **exit / voice / loyalty** (political-science): leaving versus complaining as feedback channels in declining organizations, with loyalty raising the use of voice (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[clark-2017-principles/ch03-politics]] (pp. 50–51): here the citizen's three responses to a harmful state action: adapt (exit), try to reverse it (voice), or accept it (loyalty). Applied to citizens and states, extending Hirschman's firm–consumer setting
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch06-economic-p2]] (pp. 187, 191): here a game between Crown and Parliamentarians where exit means disinvesting, voice means petitioning for limits, and loyalty means continuing to invest
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch10-varieties-p3]] (p. 392, n. 32): large W/S means members have credible exit threats
- [[clark-2017-principles/ch15-institutional-p1]] (p. 689): only a back-reference: exit option as in "voting with one's feet"
