### analytic-philosophy

- [[popper-1959-logic/appii-untitled]] (pp. 321, 323): $p(x_1,x_2)$, "probability of $x_1$ with regard to $x_2$"; primitive in $s_1$, defined by a ratio in $s_2$ [new]
- [[popper-1959-logic/appiii-untitled]] (p. 326): $p(a,b)$: probability of $a$ given $b$, or of finding an $a$ among the $b$'s [new]
- [[popper-1959-logic/appiv-untitled-2]] (pp. 336–337, 342): p(a,b) taken as basic, absolute probability defined from it (Postulate AP or $p(a) = p(a,\overline{\bar a a})$), so zero-probability conditions are allowed [alias `relative-probability-primitive`; new]
- [[popper-1959-logic/appv-untitled-2]] (pp. 356, 363): $p(a,b)$, the probability of $a$ given $b$, is basic; it can be defined even when $b$ has probability 0 [alias `relative-probability-primitive`; new]
