# coordination-game-multiple-equilibria

## Canon spine entry

- **coordination game / multiple equilibria** (economics-finance): games where agents gain by matching each other, so self-fulfilling good or bad outcomes are possible (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[arthur-1994-elfarol/main]] (p. 408, 410): Arthur says the reader may see the bar problem as applying to commons or coordination problems with limits to desired coordination; he says multiple consistent sets of hypotheses are more often found than a unique one [as `coordination game / multiple equilibria`]
- [[morris-2002-public/main]] (p. 1524): here a coordination motive exists, but equilibrium is unique for all parameters [as `coordination game / multiple equilibria`]
- [[stiglitz-2002-paradigm/main-p3]] (pp. 521–522): multiple equilibria, some Pareto dominating others, could easily arise; consequences include a high level of indeterminacy and that government intervention can sometimes move the economy between equilibria, after which continued intervention might not be needed [as `coordination game / multiple equilibria`]
