# social-capital

## Canon spine entry

- **social capital** (political-science): trust, norms of reciprocity, and networks of civic engagement that make collective action easier (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[clark-2017-principles/ch07-cultural-p1]] (p. 219): reciprocity norms and civic networks, which Putnam treats as civic culture
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch02-institutional]] (p. 36): shared norms that reduce the cost of monitoring and sanctioning
- [[ostrom-1990-commons/ch06-framework]] (p. 184): shared norms and patterns of reciprocity built over time. Appropriators use them to build institutions
