# necessary-a-posteriori

## Canon spine entry

- **necessary a posteriori** (analytic-philosophy): truths that could not have been otherwise but are known only by investigation ("water is H2O") (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[kripke-1980-naming/addenda]] (p. 159): necessary truths known empirically. In Kripke's cases analysis shows they cannot be contingently true
- [[kripke-1980-naming/ch01-lecture-p1]] (p. 38): announced as claim to be argued later [as `necessary a posteriori`]
- [[kripke-1980-naming/ch02-lecture]] (pp. 99, 101, 104): here, name-identities and theoretical identities held necessary though not known a priori [as `necessary a posteriori`]
- [[kripke-1980-naming/ch03-lecture-p1]] (pp. 108–110, 125): a truth, such as an identity between names or a kind's composition, true in all worlds yet found out empirically [as `necessary a posteriori`]
- [[kripke-1980-naming/ch03-lecture-p2]] (p. 140): true in all possible worlds, but known only by empirical investigation; theoretical identities such as heat = molecular motion [as `necessary a posteriori`]
- [[putnam-1975-meaning/main]] (p. 151): metaphysically necessary and epistemically contingent [as `necessary a posteriori`]
