# rational-agent-assumption

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch02-true]] (pp. 17, 20–21): start from ideal rationality (believe implications, no contradictions) and revise downward; attribute what the system *ought* to have
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch03-three]] (pp. 49–50): beliefs and desires are those a system ought to have; behaviour is what would be rational; a "myth" that works because we are pretty rational
- [[premack-1978-chimpanzee/main]] (p. 518): the authors' proposal: the chimpanzee assumes the actor wants the goal and knows how to reach it
