# tipping-point

## Canon spine entry

- **tipping point** (complex-systems): a threshold beyond which a system shifts rapidly and often irreversibly to another state (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch06-genetics]] (pdf 119–120): Mitchell's quoted label for the burst of progress in genetics that began with the 1953 double helix
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch16-applying]] (p. 123): Gladwell's term here: process accelerating in a positive-feedback cycle, or accelerating failures
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 53): threshold where a system shifts abruptly to a contrasting state; in models such thresholds correspond to bifurcations [as `tipping point`]
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p2]] (pp. 31–32): Schelling's "tipping": a minority's entry causes cumulative evacuation; often no clear single point, and none need occur at the modal tolerance [as `tipping point`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p2]] (p. 78): here the sudden jump from low to high population (an "outbreak") when a fixed point disappears
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p3]] (p. 291): in 8.1.15, a critical fraction $p_c$ of committed believers at which the final state jumps
