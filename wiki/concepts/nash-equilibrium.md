# nash-equilibrium

## Canon spine entry

- **Nash equilibrium** (decision-game-theory): a profile in which everyone is best-responding to everyone else (UG)

- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch02-nash-p2]] (p. Prop. 34.1, p. 34): here characterized as: every player's action lies in her best-response set against the others' actions
