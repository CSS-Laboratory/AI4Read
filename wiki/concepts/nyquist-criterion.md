# nyquist-criterion

## Canon spine entry

- **Nyquist criterion** (control-theory): a graphical test of closed-loop stability from the open-loop frequency response (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### control-theory

- [[astrom-2021-feedbacksystems/ch10-frequency]] (p. 10-6, 10-12): stability from encirclements of $-1$ by the image of $L$ along the Nyquist contour; simplified (Thm 10.1) and general (Thm 10.3, $Z=N+P$) forms
