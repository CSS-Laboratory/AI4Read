# homophily-heterophily

## Canon spine entry

- **Homophily / heterophily** (communication-theory): similarity or difference between communicating individuals; homophily eases communication but limits novelty (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### communication-theory

- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch10-marketing]] (p. 238): same-status pairing resembles homophily; the term is not used in this unit (canon link; not stated in this unit)
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch11-fashion]] (pp. 258, 264, 267): the authors expect and find similar age and status between influential and influencee (word not used here)
- [[katz-1955-personalinfluence/ch12-public]] (pp. 278, 286, 292): much advice-seeking stays within one's own status or age group, with notable cross-status or old-to-young flow
