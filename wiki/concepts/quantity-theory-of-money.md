# quantity-theory-of-money

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[keynes-1936-general/ch15-psychological]] (§ IV): $MV = OP$, obtained when $M_2 = 0$ (static society or no uncertainty about future rates); Keynes calls it "much the same" as the traditional quantity theory [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch20-employment]] (§ III, para. 2): beyond full employment, in strict equilibrium, the crude quantity theory holds: prices rise in proportion to $MV$ [new]
- [[keynes-1936-general/ch21-theory]] (§ III): in Keynes's simple statement, employment changes in proportion to money under unemployment and prices at full employment; he reaches it by adding simplifying assumptions [new]
