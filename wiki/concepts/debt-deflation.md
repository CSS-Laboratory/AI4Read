# debt-deflation

## Canon spine entry

- **debt deflation** (economics-finance): falling prices raise real debt burdens, which forces asset sales and causes further price falls (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### economics-finance

- [[keynes-1936-general/ch19-changes]]: here: falling wages and prices raise real debt, with insolvency risk. Keynes does not use the term (§ II, item (7) and difference (iii))
- [[minsky-1992-instability/main]] (p. 1): a self-feeding contraction, with Fisher cited as the classic description [as `debt deflation`]
- [[shiller-2019-narrative/ch12-gold]] (p. 157; canon link; the term is not used in this unit): Shiller only notes that deflation raised debts' real value and hurt debtors
