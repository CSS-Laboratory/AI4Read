# satisficing

## Canon spine entry

- **satisficing** (decision-game-theory): stopping search at the first option that meets an aspiration level (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[simon-1955-behavioral/main]] (p. 106): seeking an alternative whose payoff meets an aspiration level, then stopping. Simon does not use the word here, only "satisfactory" and "good enough"

### theory-of-mind

- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch07-intentional]] (p. 264): Simon’s idea that nature settles for good enough; Dennett says it can often be shown optimal when search costs are added [new]
