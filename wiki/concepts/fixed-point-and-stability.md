# fixed-point-and-stability

## Canon spine entry

- **fixed point and stability** (complex-systems): a state that does not change, and whether small perturbations die out or grow (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p1]] (p. 13): majority-rule fixed points $0,\tfrac12,1$ ($\tfrac12$ unstable)
- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p2]] (pp. 31, 34): stable/unstable fixed points in Eqs. (36), (37) [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[granovetter-1978-threshold/main]] (pp. 1420, 1428–1429): here the equilibrium $r_e$ solves $F(r)=r$. The author's "stability" is the resistance of the equilibrium to changes in the threshold distribution
- [[lorenz-1963-nonperiodic/main]] (pp. 132, 135): steady states of (25)–(27) and the definitions of stable and unstable trajectories [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[mitchell-2009-guidedtour/ch02-dynamics]] (pdf 50): a value that, once reached, never changes, e.g. 0.5 at $R=2$ [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 55): Box 2 shows stability via the sign of $\partial f/\partial x$ at equilibrium [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p1]] (pp. 148, 150): an arrangement in which nobody wants to move; complete segregation can be stable
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p2]] (pp. 20, 22): stable equilibria are curve intersections or one-color corners; stability against small or large perturbations [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch01-overview]] (pp. 10–11): fixed points listed as the $n=1$ nonlinear phenomenon; not defined here [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch02-flows]] (pp. 17, 19): a point where $f(x^*)=0$; stable if small disturbances die out [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p1]] (p. 48): a state with $f(x^*)=0$, stable or unstable by sign of $f'(x^*)$
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p2]] (p. 77): stability alternates along the $x$-axis; $a$ stable, $b$ unstable, $c$ stable
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch04-flows]] (p. 99): here $\theta^*$ with $f(\theta^*)=0$, classified by sign of $f'$ [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch06-phase-p1]] (p. 147): $\mathbf f(\mathbf x^*)=\mathbf 0$; steady state whose stability depends on nearby flow
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch06-phase-p2]] (p. 179): the author stresses that the index is not related to stability: stable and unstable nodes both have index +1 [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch07-limit-p1]] (p. 199, 210): used for $r^*=0,1$ and for the repeller test via trace and determinant [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch07-limit-p2]] (p. 224): used on the amplitude equation $r'=\tfrac18r(4-r^2)$: $r^*=0$ unstable, $r^*=2$ stable [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p3]] (p. 287): in the cycle setting, the stability of a periodic state is read from its characteristic multipliers; here they all equal 1, which gives neutral stability
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p1]] (pp. 322–324): origin and C± classified as sinks, saddles; stability conditions on r [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch09-lorenz-p2]] (p. 335): for a map, $z^*$ with $f(z^*)=z^*$; unstable if $|f'(z^*)|>1$ [as `fixed point and stability`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch10-one-p1]] (p. 357): $f(x^*)=x^*$; stable if $|f'(x^*)|<1$, unstable if $>1$, marginal at $=1$
