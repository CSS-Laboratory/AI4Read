# political-awareness-sophistication

## Canon spine entry

- **political awareness / sophistication** (political-science): attention to and knowledge of politics. It moderates both exposure to persuasion and resistance to it (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch11-groups]] (p. 310): in this chapter, more engaged citizens show more partisan bias, and sophistication does not guarantee better judgment
- [[chong-2007-framing/main]] (p. 112): "knowledge" as moderator; effects mixed unless prior attitudes are controlled
- [[converse-1964-beliefsystems/ch01-introduction]] (p. 16): here approximated by information, education and involvement, which decline down the levels

- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch08-tests]] (pp. 151, 166–167): habitual attention to and knowledge of politics. Raises reception and, for value-inconsistent messages, resistance
