# physical-design-stance

## Canon spine entry

- **physical / design stance** (theory-of-mind): Dennett's alternative strategies: predict from physics, or from what the system was designed to do (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch02-true]] (pp. 16–17): physical stance: predict from physical constitution and laws; design stance: predict from what it was designed to do
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch03-three]] (p. 73): the intentional stance as a limiting case of the design stance: assume only that the design is optimal
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch04-making]] (p. 109): predicting frog behaviour from physics or from functional design; gives "hegemony" over intentional predictions in principle [as `physical / design stance`]
- [[dennett-1987-intentional-stance/ch06-styles]] (p. 221): the calculator was designed by engineers to honour arithmetic, without representing it
