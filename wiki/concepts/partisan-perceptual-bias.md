# partisan-perceptual-bias

## Canon spine entry

- **partisan perceptual bias** (political-science): partisans perceive objective conditions (economy, war) differently, so beliefs need not converge with evidence (grad)

## Definitions in the sources

### political-science

- [[achen-2016-realists/ch01-democratic]] (p. 17): party preference colors perceptions of facts and policy stands
- [[achen-2016-realists/ch04-rational]] (pp. 95–96, 107): partisans see conditions differently, so perceived economic conditions need not match objective ones
- [[achen-2016-realists/ch10-it]] (pp. 272, 277–284): partisans see issue positions and "objective" facts through party loyalty
- [[iyengar-2012-affect/main]] (pdf 24): partisans see the economy differently; given as a consequence of affect, not tested here
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch10-information-p2]] (p. 251): here a constant tendency to accept congenial and resist uncongenial messages, assumed at 67/33 for illustration
- [[zaller-1992-massopinion/ch11-evaluating-p2]] (p. 297): strong partisan differences in character assessments, increasing with awareness
