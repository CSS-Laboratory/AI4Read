# temporo-parietal-junction-tpj

## Canon spine entry

- **temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)** (theory-of-mind): cortical region, especially the right TPJ, selectively engaged when representing others' beliefs (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### theory-of-mind

- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch04-evidence]] (pp. 66–67): here split into R-TPJ (mental content) and L-TPJ (perspectives in general, also signs)
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch06-cognitive]] (p. 110): right TPJ is selective for beliefs and desires, left TPJ especially for perspectives; cognitive processes unclear
- [[apperly-2011-mindreaders/ch07-elaborating]] (p. 170): R-TPJ selectivity read as part of high-level processing or as a low-level module triggered by unsuitable stimuli
- [[heyes-2014-cultural-evolution/main]] (p. 1; Fig. 2, p. 3): part of the network [as `temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)`]
- [[saxe-2003-tpj/main]] (p. 1): cortical region; the TPJ-M subregion is proposed to be selective for mental-state reasoning [as `temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)`]
