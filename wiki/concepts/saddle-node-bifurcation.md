## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p3]] (p. 42): in the $\sigma$-modified model, two stable fixed points coexist above a critical density
- [[scheffer-2009-earlywarning/main]] (p. 53, Box 1 p. 54): equilibrium curve folded backwards; the unstable middle branch marks the basin border; dominant eigenvalue becomes zero at the fold points F1, F2 [as `saddle-node (fold) bifurcation`; alias `saddle-node-fold-bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p1]] (pp. 46–48): two fixed points collide and vanish. Normal form $\dot x=r\pm x^2$. Also called fold, turning-point or blue-sky bifurcation [alias `saddle-node-fold-bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch03-bifurcations-p2]] (p. 71, p. 77): two fixed points collide and vanish where a line is tangent to a curve; here along $h=\pm h_c(r)$ and the budworm curves [alias `saddle-node-fold-bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch04-flows]] (p. 99): two fixed points collide at $a=\omega$ (or $\gamma=1$, $\mu=1$, $I=I_c$) and vanish [as `saddle-node (fold) bifurcation`; alias `saddle-node-fold-bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p1]] (pp. 245, 248): two fixed points collide and vanish; in 2D the action lies on the unstable manifold of the saddle
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p2]] (pp. 264–265): two fixed points (here also two cycles) merge and vanish; cycles are born with O(1) amplitude
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch08-bifurcations-p3]] (p. 298): fixed points collide and vanish; also of cycles, in the averaged forced Duffing system [alias `saddle-node-fold-bifurcation`]
- [[strogatz-2024-nonlinear/ch10-one-p1]] (p. 371): author states a tangent bifurcation is this under another name
