# paradigm

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[kuhn-1962-structure/ch10-revolutions]] (pp. 112, 122): shared framework that fixes what counts as a datum, instrument and relevant concept; here also what is seen (pp. 112, 122). *(canon: "paradigm / normal science")*
