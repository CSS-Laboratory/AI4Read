# conversational-implicature

## Canon spine entry

- **conversational implicature** (analytic-philosophy): what a speaker conveys beyond what they literally say, inferred via the maxims; it can be cancelled (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### analytic-philosophy

- [[grice-1975-conversation/main]] (pp. 49–50): what a speaker conveys beyond what he says, derived by the hearer from the CP and maxims by a calculation [as `conversational implicature`]
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch07-psychological]] (p. 98): mentioned only: Grice treats metaphor as a species of it [as `conversational implicature`]
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch11-semantic]] (p. 146): merely conveyed, not explicitly said; Lycan says it cannot supply what is *said* [as `conversational implicature`]
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch13-implicative]] (p. 165, 167): what is conveyed via the hearer's reasoning from CP and maxims; worked out and cancelable [as `conversational implicature`]
- [[lycan-2019-language/ch14-expressive]] (pp. 185–186): Grice's model; here shown inadequate for irony and sarcasm [as `conversational implicature`]
