# threshold-model

## Canon spine entry

- **threshold model** (complex-systems): each agent acts once enough others have acted, so cascades depend on the threshold distribution (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### complex-systems

- [[castellano-2009-socialdynamics/main-p3]] (pp. 40, 46): units activate above a critical neighbor density (Mexican wave); rumor models have none
- [[granovetter-1978-threshold/main]] (p. 1422): each actor acts once the share (or number) of others acting reaches a personal threshold; outcome follows from the threshold distribution [new]
- [[schelling-1971-segregation/main-p2]] (p. 17): each person stays only while the other-color ratio is within a personal limit; outcomes depend on the distribution of limits [as `threshold model`]

### communication-theory

- [[rogers-2003-diffusion/ch08-diffusion-p2]] (pdf 410): each person has a resistance level overcome gradually by network influence [new]
