# iterated-elimination-of-dominated-strategies

## Canon spine entry

- **iterated elimination of dominated strategies** (decision-game-theory): repeatedly deleting dominated strategies, assuming common knowledge of rationality (UG)

## Definitions in the sources

### decision-game-theory

- [[dixit-2008-art-strategy/ch04-beautiful]] (pp. 124–125): drop dominated and never-best-response strategies in successive rounds; a unique remainder is a Nash equilibrium
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch04-mixed-p2]] (p. 132): repeated removal of strictly dominated actions; surviving profiles include all Nash equilibria
- [[osborne-2004-introduction/ch12-rationalizability]] (p. 357): repeatedly deleting dominated actions. Here the strict version equals rationalizability
- [[schelling-1960-conflict/appc-re-p2]] (pp. 369–370): discarding dominated rows and columns; Schelling notes the order can affect which cells remain
