# eight-schools

## Canon entry

- **Eight schools** (bayesian): coaching effects in eight schools: the textbook demonstration of hierarchical partial pooling between "no pooling" and "complete pooling" — gelman-2013-bda3 (Ch. 5)

## Descriptions in the sources

### bayesian

- [[gelman-2013-bda3/appc-computation]] (pp. 591–608): hierarchical normal model of eight coaching effects, fitted in C.2–C.4 and revisited in C.5
- [[gelman-2013-bda3/ch05-hierarchical]] (pp. 119–123, 130–131): SAT coaching; separate vs pooled vs hierarchical
- [[gelman-2013-bda3/ch06-model]] (pp. 159–161): model checks and sensitivity analysis of the SAT coaching model
- [[gelman-2013-bda3/ch07-evaluating]] (pp. 178–184): no pooling vs complete pooling vs hierarchical compared by AIC/DIC/WAIC/CV (Table 7.1); Bayes factor failure
